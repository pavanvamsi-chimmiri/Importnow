Let's ImportNow

China to India import website — project summary

1. What this project is

Let's ImportNow is a full website for importing goods from China to India. A client chooses the product. The team finds verified factories, inspects them in person, checks quality, ships the container, clears customs and GST in India, and unloads at the shop, warehouse, or villa.

The site was designed and developed as a complete product: branding, layout, copy, animations, mobile navigation, a working quote form, and a live deployment. The visual identity uses China red, India saffron, and India green on an ocean-navy background. Cargo ships with Chinese and Indian flags appear in the hero and on delivered-job cards.

3. What we designed and developed

  1. Created the Let's ImportNow brand: logo, tricolor bar, ocean theme, and a custom ship favicon in the browser tab (not the default Next.js “N”).
  2. Built the site in Next.js, React, TypeScript, and Tailwind CSS, with reusable components for header, footer, logo, ships, marquee, and sections.
  3. Illustrated a China cargo ship (red hull, Chinese flag, CHINA on the side) and an India cargo ship (green hull, tricolor stripe, Indian flag) sailing between origin and destination ports.
  4. Coded every homepage section listed below, using the real service copy: sourcing hubs, licence details, trip inclusions, client stories, and FAQs.
  5. Wired interactions: smooth-scroll menu, mobile hamburger, FAQ accordion, journey step player with day counter, and a quote form that sends the filled details.
  6. Made the layout work on desktop, tablet, and phone with no overlapping text and a working mobile menu.
  7. Deployed the production site and published the source on GitHub.

Three testimonials and six questions on MOQ, delivery time, GST, IEC, factory visits, and business ideas.

Contact and footer

Quote form (name, city, category, quantity, budget, details), office line Hyderabad · Shanghai, and site footer.

5. Features that work on the live site

  • Menu links scroll to Services, How it works, What we import, China trips, Contact, and FAQ.
  • Get a free quote buttons take the visitor to the contact form.
  • The contact form collects sourcing details and opens a message with those fields filled in.
  • FAQ items open and close. The first question starts expanded.
  • How it works steps play through automatically and can be clicked. The progress bar shows origin, destination, and day.
  • City names scroll in a marquee. Hover states and button transitions are in place.
  • Phone, tablet, and desktop layouts were checked so the page does not break or scroll sideways.

6. Visual work

The old default tab icon was replaced with a cargo-ship mark in China red and India green. Headings use Sora; body text uses Inter. Cards sit on glass-style panels with a gold edge. The hero globe shows China and India cities and a trade route. Project cards no longer use a plain purple block; they show two labelled ships on water with origin and destination chips (for example Foshan → Villa, Yiwu → Adilabad).

7. Technology we used

Next.js 16 (App Router), React 19, TypeScript, Tailwind CSS 4, Framer Motion, Lucide React, Google fonts Sora and Inter. The app is organised into src/app, src/components, src/sections, src/lib, and src/types. Content lives in src/lib/constants.ts so services, industries, FAQs, and stories can be updated in one place. The production build is hosted on Vercel from the GitHub repository.

2. What we have done

Let's ImportNow is the China-to-India import website: sourcing, factory inspection, shipping, customs, and doorstep delivery through a registered company in Shanghai. This update adds Sign in and Sign up and keeps the same homepage look on those pages.

Account pages

/signup and /signin. Left side: homepage headline, China and India cargo ships (Shanghai → Chennai), and stats. Right side: the form.

Header and menu

Sign in and Sign up in the desktop header and the mobile menu. After login: Hi, {name} and Sign out.

How accounts work

Name, email, city, password. Duplicate email, short password, and wrong password show errors. Session stays in the browser.

Same brand as home

Tricolor bar, ocean-navy background, cargo-ship tab icon, Sora headings, Inter body, China-red / India-saffron / India-green buttons.

6. How to use Sign in and Sign up

  1. Open the website and click Sign up in the header or mobile menu.
  2. Enter full name, email, city in India, and a password of at least 8 characters.
  3. After create, the home header shows Hi, {first name} and Sign out.
  4. Sign out, then Sign in with the same email and password. A wrong password shows an error.
